Read this before adding any content (people, places, items, factions, dungeons, lines of text). It exists so that
expansions feel like the same world written by the same author.

This folder is the *what exists and what it's like*; those are the *how it reads* and *how it works*.

## Status markers

- Unmarked text is **canon** (decided by the author or already in the game).
- **PROPOSED** means drafted by Claude to fill a gap and not yet approved. Treat as a strong default, not law, and
  flag it if you build on it. When the author confirms or changes one, remove the marker.
- **OPEN** is a question nobody has answered. Don't invent an answer in content; ask.

## The hard rules (never break these)

1. **Blackbird always hates Atlas and opposes everything it does.** She can be a terrible hero, a vain one, a
   greedy one, even actively evil depending on the run, but she never works for Atlas, never admires it and never
   is neutral about it. Her *methods* and *motives* vary; her enemy doesn't.
2. **Nothing truly grim.** No atrocity played for pathos, no gore for its own sake. Violence is cartoonish, death is
   a rebuild, victims are never the punchline. The darkest the setting gets is *absurd* menace.
3. **Punch up, not down.** Jokes land on Atlas, the brass, the rich, and Blackbird's ego. Never on the poor,
   minorities, the disabled, or victims. A street kid, a janitor or a bartender can be funny; they are never the
   butt of the joke.
4. **Invented brands only.** No real companies, products, parties, countries or celebrities.
5. **Rescued people have their own agency.** Damsels (hackers, bartenders, fixers, medics) are competent people with
   goals, not prizes. They can refuse, rescue themselves, and be annoyed at being "rescued".
6. **Adult, not explicit.** Innuendo yes, blow-by-blow no (see TONE.md).
